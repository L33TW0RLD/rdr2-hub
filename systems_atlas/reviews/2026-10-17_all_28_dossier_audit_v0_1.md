### Priority interpretation

- **P0 — Preservation / irreversibility gate:** An unsupported certainty could lose a scarce item, optional camp/story event, medal, quest reward, money, Honor or limited opportunity. Consumer must be conditional, evidence-bound, and spoiler-aware until resolved.
- **P1 — Gameplay/official-progress correctness gate:** Needs targeted tracker/menu/platform confirmation before detailed directions or exact credit claims; ordinary safe discussion can proceed with caveats.
- **P2 — Low-stakes precision / presentation gate:** Usually safe to describe broad system, but edition/version/current interface or exhaustive optional details still need validation before being exact.

## Sampled external-source reconciliation (not a full source recrawl)

**S01 — Official Story Mode patch behavior.** [Rockstar Title Update 1.15 notes](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/28AmlyPGLVKsJ8OpdN8myn/red-dead-redemption-2-title-update-1-15-notes-ps4-xbox-one-pc-stadia) explicitly describe Story Mode field-camp fast travel *after purchase*, replay availability of *currently owned weapons*, controller fishing Hold to Reel, and Photo Mode/console rollout specifics. This supports D11, D24, D16 and D28 narrow statements; it does not certify a current Windows keybind, arbitrary save persistence or Online pamphlet ownership. The support article's **page update date** is not the feature's original implementation date.

**S02 — Narrow lost-legendary-pelt guarantee.** [Rockstar lost pelts support](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/480idh1zECJ0ojrheApV1U/unable-to-skin-legendary-animals-or-losing-their-pelts-in-red-dead-redemption-2) states unusable/lost legendary pelts/carcasses reach **Trapper inventory after a short delay**. Does **not explicitly** promise recovered sale cash, unrelated ordinary perfect pelts, or all Fence-specific components. D17's conservative framing is supported.

**S03 — Genuine price conflict even within one secondary domain.** [RDR2.org Pearson Crafting](https://www.rdr2.org/guides/story-mode/pearson-crafting-guide/) lists Leather Working Tools **$180** while [RDR2.org Camp Upgrades](https://www.rdr2.org/guides/camp-upgrades-guide/) lists **$225**. [Tech Advisor](https://www.techadvisor.com/article/732834/how-to-get-leather-working-tools-upgrade-in-red-dead-redemption-2.html) agrees with $225; [Twinfinite](https://twinfinite.net/guides/red-dead-redemption-2-leather-working-tools-how-get-what-they-do/) lists $180. D02's base price is a **guide-backed default, not a universal menu fact**; D03 rightfully preserves the conflict. Confirm using in-game Ledger before irreversible purchase recommendations.

**S04 — Official 100% collectibles source disagreement.** [PowerPyx broad checklist](https://www.powerpyx.com/red-dead-redemption-2-100-guide-checklist/) enumerates **144 cards**; [GameFAQs detailed Total Completion](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/xboxone/200180-red-dead-redemption-2/faqs/76594/total-completion) states **one complete 12-card set mailed** for the official row; [Red Dead Wiki 100%](https://reddead.fandom.com/wiki/100%25_Completion_%28RDR2%29) agrees with one set. D15/D20 correctly protect against a broad-summary overcount, but exact player checkbox remains the consumer proof.

**S05 — Nine challenge families / three Bluegill.** [RDR2.org challenges article](https://www.rdr2.org/wiki/challenges/) mistakenly introduces **eight** challenge tracks but omits Explorer from that first sentence, while the in-game-compatible [PowerPyx official 100% checklist](https://www.powerpyx.com/red-dead-redemption-2-100-guide-checklist/) and [Red Dead Wiki 100%](https://reddead.fandom.com/wiki/100%25_Completion_%28RDR2%29) require **nine** completed strands; RDR2.org's Survivalist table also specifies **three Bluegill**, agreeing with D27's cautious rank-1 wording. Reconcile per objective; a popular guide domain is not sufficient authority for every nearby claim.

**S06 — Camp companion achievement vs ordinary table game.** [XboxAchievements Friends With Benefits checklist](https://www.xboxachievements.com/game/red-dead-redemption-2/achievement/160871-Friends-With-Benefits.html) names marked camp activities per relevant chapter; [TrueAchievements player reports](https://www.trueachievements.com/a262346/friends-with-benefits-achievement) conflict on whether unmarked games count. D05/D14/D23's safe guidance is to use an **actual marked qualifying companion activity**, not an unqualified table-game encounter.

**Independent verification limit:** These six sampled cross-claims show **properly qualified research is generally fit as conditional explanatory content**. They are not six exhaustive quantitative test suites; no controlled Story Mode run, Gold medal replay, game patch matrix, companion UI client or current player-save readback was performed.
