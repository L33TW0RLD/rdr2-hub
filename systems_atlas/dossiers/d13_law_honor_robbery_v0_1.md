## 1. Definitions, player interaction, and observable feedback [T0/T1]

**Honor is not a criminal-history meter.** Rockstar explicitly describes Honor as responding to merciful/constructive actions versus cruelty, harm to innocents and theft; reputation has dialog, pricing and narrative effects. A crime can be *unobserved* and still morally dishonorable. A player can also remain liable for an earlier regional bounty despite subsequent helpful actions. Inspect **Player/Honor display** and the transient Honor icon instead of assigning invented precise point counts. [E01][E02][E03][E04]

**The crime-response sequence is contextual, not one atomic “wanted = bounty” flag:**
1. **Incident:** action may be harmless, hostile, criminal, provoked, scripted, or witnessed; the game classifies it in context. Some nearby NPCs fight rather than report.
2. **Witness:** an eye/eye-witness indicator can appear on radar; visible witness may flee toward law or react aggressively. A player's choice to threaten is a *chance*, not guaranteed compliance and can aggravate danger; attacking the witness is a new consequential action. [E05][E06]
3. **Report / investigation:** `Wanted` display may show **investigating** or **unknown suspect** when responding lawmen know of a crime without identifying Arthur. A witness can report *an event* without reliably naming him. Lawmen can discover a crime scene themselves. [E05][E06][E07]
4. **Identification / pursuit:** `Wanted: Arthur Morgan` or equivalent name, red search region and pursuers indicate personal recognition; serious crimes intensify responses. Surrender may be offered for eligible confrontations, but is not universally available once shooting starts. [E05][E07]
5. **Persistent bounty / cooldown:** disappearing from radar or the initial search ending does **not** necessarily remove an existing state bounty. Check map/jurisdiction, `Wanted` UI and post office separately. [E07][E08]

**Identification and face coverings:** a bandana/mask can help prevent ordinary civilians recognizing the perpetrator **if donned before** the relevant crime, but not make Arthur invisible to lawmen. An `Unknown Suspect` state is compatible with investigation. Historical outfit/recognition detail is supported largely by guides and player testing, not a universal verified formula; changes of clothes/hair/horse are **not an immunity recipe**. Original game tutorial discusses disguises; exact clothing is D10. [E05][E06][E07]

**Fists, lasso and hogtie are different acts.** A fistfight is not the same as drawing a gun. The lasso restrains a target temporarily; approach the roped target and use the displayed `Hogtie` action to immobilize for transport. This is useful when an authorized bounty poster requests delivery alive, but tying an innocent is not a lawfully sanctioned act merely because it is nonlethal. Do not infer a universal neutral Honor effect for civilians from specialist tips. [E05][E09]

**Robbery prompt vs completion credit:** a person holdup, shop till, home stash, coach stop, train passenger/baggage raid, and mission-scripted theft are not interchangeable counter events. The game's **Progress → Total Completion** category tracker, not a narrative description (“I robbed someone”), is decisive for a specific official checkbox. [E10][E11][E12]

## 4. Story, time, edition and ambiguity risks (including DISPUTED) [T0/T1]

- **High Honor ≠ “all enemies spared”**; Rockstar's positive/negative examples refer specifically to context (innocents, mercy and rescue). Whether fighting a hostile robber in self-defense triggers a wanted charge is context- and witness-dependent. Do **not** promise a universal immunity or “every hostile kill is Honor-neutral.” [E01][E03][E05]
- **Accidental horse/vehicle collision:** running into someone or interacting with a mounted NPC can create an assault, theft or other response depending on context. A mistaken prompt should not be retroactively represented as intentional robbery, nor should accidental harm be declared consequence-free. [E05][E06]; **exact event classification UNKNOWN** unless HUD observed.
- **Fear and intimidation:** brandishing a weapon, pointing a gun, striking, stealing a horse/vehicle, looting and killing may be different offenses and provoke different responses. A shop robbery is **not** necessarily any shop item taken, and a horse stolen from a passer-by is **not** the official coach robbery checkbox. [E05][E11][E14]
- **Masked identity and law:** lawmen identify despite covered face in standard descriptions. Repeated-town/outfit/hair/horse detailed recognition formulas vary by guide and patch; avoid false guarantees. [E05][E06][E07]
- **Honor deltas/caps:** exact numerical units circulating in community tables (e.g. fixed per greeting, per corpse, or payout for “three greetings”) are **not independently official across builds**; action animation/icon is safer than a fabricated +X ledger. Honor may have story-bound rank limits that this dossier does not establish for a given chapter. [E01][E03][E04]
- **Arrest limits:** lawmen might shoot immediately after severe violence or assault on officers. Standing unarmed is a prerequisite to an *offered* surrender, not a universal promise. [E05][E08]
- **Official robbery tally vs scripted missions DISPUTED:** Gamer Guides states robbery missions do not count for miscellaneous 100%, whereas GameFAQs says the `Friends in Very Low Places` stage counts as the coach type. **Do not reconcile by asserting either universal rule**. Observe the **four checkmarks** in Progress. [E15][E16]
- **Story territory/T2:** hostile `Dead or Alive` regions may remain inaccessible independently of ordinary regional bounty payment; no exploit instruction or story bypass. [E07][E08]
- **Online/Story edition distinction:** Honor resets/gold currency from RDO are excluded. No new “Patch 2026” assumed solely because a site was checked in 2026.

## 7. Three common mistakes and practical Chapter 2 examples [T0/T1]

**Mistakes**
1. **“I wore a bandana, so the law can't identify me.”** False guarantee: civilian anonymity may be limited; law officers can name Arthur even if masked. [E05–E07]
2. **“The red search area disappeared, so my bounty is paid.”** Wanted pursuit cool-down and persistent **state debt** are separate. [E05][E08]
3. **“I stole a horse / robbed four people, so all four official robbery types are done.”** Not the same categories; tracker requires coach/home/shop/train. [E10–E12]

**Example A — Valentine misunderstanding without escalation:** A passer-by reacts angrily after Arthur's horse bumps them or a context prompt was misread. **If no Wanted/witness alert appears**, simply give space; don't assume a crime or restart. **If a witness/wanted indicator appears**, holster weapons, withdraw rather than threaten/kill bystanders, watch if `Unknown Suspect` or Arthur's name appears; if a lawful surrender prompt is actually offered, the player may choose it. Preserve safety and High Honor roleplay; do not claim the bump always incurs assault or an exact fine. [E05–E08]

**Example B — helping a stranger on Heartlands road:** A civilian is actually being attacked and Arthur wants to assist. Prefer intervening against active aggressors proportionately, spare/assist the survivor where the prompt offers it, observe Honor tick and any witness context. Do not loot an innocent or assume attacking a misunderstood NPC is automatically self-defense. The outcome is *player-observed*, not an Atlas persistent achievement. [E01][E03]

**Example C — optional 100%-planning without outlaw grinding:** Arthur asks whether to pursue the four robbery types during Chapter 2 while protecting High Honor. Say: “The official tracker needs **coach, home, shop and train**, separately. You do not need to clear them now. We can check which category is marked in Progress and return to story or a peaceful outing.” Do not send him to a future Chapter 3 contact, teach a bounty-avoidance exploit, assume a mission counted, or compel a morally incompatible activity. [E10][E11][E15][E16]

## 10. Precise GameGuide and Free Roam consumer handoff

**Minimum read-only intake when asked “What do I do about the law?”** Confirm whether a genuine `Wanted` overlay is active, whether it says `Unknown Suspect` or Arthur's name, whether a witness icon remains, whether a region shows a debt, and whether the player wants High Honor/zero escalation. Ask for screenshot only if the distinction changes the next action, not automatically.

- **When there is no active Wanted evidence:** do **not** invent a crime/amount; return to current activity, with brief caution that robbery can cost Honor.
