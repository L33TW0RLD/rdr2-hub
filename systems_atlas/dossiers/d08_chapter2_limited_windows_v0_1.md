## 1. Definitions, interactions and feedback (T0)

- **Story Honor opportunity**: optional authored Story mission or stage contributing to the separate **Lending a Hand** achievement, sometimes shown as white marker or letter. The actual mission's appearance in **Progress > Story** differs from ordinary **Stranger** strands marked with a white question mark. Verify title and in-game log before classifying [E01][E03].
- **Optional medal-bearing stage**: an avoidable Story segment with a Gold objective; a subsequent story event can replace or close the original segment. **Pouring Forth Oil II** is the key Chapter 2 example [E06][E07].
- **Camp companion activity**: a marked invitation potentially contributing to **Friends With Benefits**, defined precisely by read-only Wave 1 D05. An ordinary table game is not automatic credit [E01][R01].
- **Item favor**: character requests a named item; it becomes a task and must be handed to the recipient for **Errand Boy**. Owning an item or speaking alone doesn't count [E01][R01].
- **Ordinary Stranger/encounter**: map question mark, authored ambient event or staged optional quest. Some can be completed after story, a few have exceptions; the question-mark shape itself isn't an expiry proof [E10][E11].
- **Chapter exit**: campaign story advances beyond Horseshoe Overlook; actual observed late Chapter 2 progression may close camp-specific opportunities, with some earlier timing uncertain [E17][R02].

**Observation surfaces:** exact person/marker label, letter on Arthur's table, accepted objective or task log, in-game Story log and Gold requirements, Total Completion checklist, account achievement progress where supplied, and permitted manual-save confirmation. **NOT OBSERVED** must not be mislabeled PERMANENTLY MISSED. Console controller shortcuts and PC prompts vary; obey on-screen keys, not guessed bindings.

## 4. NOW / BEFORE EXIT / CAN WAIT / DISPUTED algorithm

- **NOW:** *one particular* active, known-to-skip event documented by more than one specialist source. Currently supported Chapter 2 example: **accepted John's oil wagon task**. No automatic NOW for unrelated mission icon or guide shelf.
- **BEFORE CHAPTER EXIT:** targeted known Chapter 2 honor letters/debts, **plus D05-marked camp invitations or active Ch2 requests** when the player values corresponding goals. Activate closer scrutiny when the actual final chapter progression is approaching, not on arbitrary days.
- **CAN WAIT:** general world exploration, basic four-game achievement, most Stranger strands, collections without a one-off encounter currently activated, or later-expiring content.
- **DISPUTED:** sources conflict on Good, Honest, Snake Oil deadline, Mary's exact appearance gate, oil-wagon hours, optional honor label for wagon, Pearson trigger, and specific NPC pre-packing cutoff. State uncertainty and use observable in-game prompts; no fabricated timer.

**Conflict of duties:** once a player-observed NOW mission stage is active, avoid delaying it for pelt runs, camp upgrades, Stranger checklist or companion requests. The player may deliberately waive a goal; GameGuide must honor that choice and independently confirm any completed activity/save, not Atlas.
