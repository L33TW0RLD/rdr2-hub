## 1. Definitions, interactions, observable proof [T0]

Story Mode has **nine strands, ten ranks each**: Bandit, Explorer, Gambler, Herbalist, Horseman, Master Hunter, Sharpshooter, Survivalist and Weapons Expert (90 objective ranks). **Within a strand ranks unlock in order**. The game can reveal a track only after its initiating action/gate, so “missing” in a menu is not proof the content is missable or completed. Multiple strands can have independently active ranks. [R01][R02][R03]

**Action is not credit.** A bird kill can affect Compendium and loot without satisfying a Sharpshooter stage that has not been activated; yarrow picked before Herbalist 1/9 cannot be assumed to carry forward. Likewise a coach stolen for a story objective, a poker hand won, or a pelt donated is not necessarily challenge credit. Open **Pause → Progress → Challenges → strand → active rank**, and inspect the counter *after* the specific action; **Total Completion** is a different view. Any UI checklist generated by the Hub is planning-only. [R01][R03][D12][D15]

**Recommended evidence packet:** (a) active rank text + progress before action; (b) relevant in-game action/result or transaction receipt; (c) same rank counter afterwards and following-rank appearance; (d) if the action is materially irreversible, current save evidence separately. Screenshots show **observations**, not independently durable GameGuide progress until recorded through its authorized process.

## 2. Conditional activation, rank gates and exceptions [T0/T1]

**General condition:** `Challenge(strand, N) REQUIRES challenge(strand, N-1) completed` for ranks 2–10; rank 1 needs the strand's *actual initiation trigger*, which may be activity-specific. Historical matching actions do **not** satisfy unseen future ranks retrospectively. Only live menu evidence confirms current rank and current counter. [R01][R03]

| Strand | Practical initial action / access issue | What must stay unknown until inspected |
|---|---|---|
| Bandit | Eligible holdup/robbery opens outlaw track; coach/wagon/horse **fence** services are not interchangeable | Active stage, whether robbery classified as successful, applicable seller actually unlocked |
| Explorer | Obtain first relevant treasure map, then qualifying treasure-map-chain discoveries | Whether a particular map/chest counts and whether a later rank was active |
| Gambler | Poker wins and table-game access; blackjack/dominoes/FFF availability varies by place and advancement | Win vs participation, location requirement, uninterrupted streak |
| Herbalist | Pick six yarrow for stage 1; some recipe gates and plant habitats later | Pick vs consume/craft/use, learned recipe, stage-9 species already credited |
| Horseman | Mounted rabbit kills; rides count only under their active timed stage | Horse's condition, starting line, timer, water disqualification |
| Master Hunter | Skin three deer; ranks later depend on distinct weapon/animal/bait conditions | Species recognition, clean-kill/pelt quality, stage-10 legendary appearance |
| Sharpshooter | Flying bird kills; later Dead Eye / long-scope / train prerequisites | Ability stage, eligible enemy/animal, consecutiveness |
| Survivalist | Bluegill fishing, normally following rod access via the Chapter 2 fishing introduction | Actual rod, tutorial/gate and whether first stage is visible |
| Weapons Expert | Eligible enemy knife kills; later ammunition, throwing tools, long-barrel service | Weapon variant, enemy classification, consecutive/one-hit wording |

**No silent prerequisite promotion:** a helpful horse, improved firearm, recipe or tonics can make a rank easier without being a *hard* unlock gate. Source-backed specific tools are required only when the rank itself calls for them; story gates are conditional, not player-state flags. [R03][R04][D12][D13]

## 3. Rewards, equipment purchase, costs, recoverability [T0/T1]

Ranks award cash/attribute XP or **unlock** particular themed **Reinforced Equipment** from the **Trapper**. **Unlock is not purchase**; bought gear is not inferred from a challenge popup. Four named pieces per strand (Holster, Off-Hand Holster, Gun Belt, Bandolier) are listed in §6. Cash and XP values in the table are **reported guide values**, not independently reproduced on this player's version or claim of payment. [R02][R11]

**Crucial distinction:** the *XP type awarded at a specific rank* is **not** always the same attribute whose **maximum cap** a complete purchased equipment set improves. Reported reinforced-gear cap affiliations: **Health** ← Horseman + Sharpshooter + Weapons Expert; **Stamina** ← Bandit + Gambler + Master Hunter; **Dead Eye** ← Explorer + Herbalist + Survivalist. At baseline, ordinary experience increases core attributes to their normal levels; completing the relevant challenges, unlocking and buying qualifying complete Trapper sets allows further maximum level increases (generally 9 after one set, 10 after all three). **Do not conclude level 10 merely from ten rank clearances**; consult `Player` level display and actual Trapper ownership. [R02][R11]

The sources describe **Legend of the East** outfit as reward for all nine strands, but actual inventory/outfit accessibility and any character-specific late-stage cases must be confirmed in Story Mode; outfit claim ≠ 100% achievement claim. [R02][R04]

**Costs and risks:** illegal Bandit acts can reduce Honor, trigger bounties or law response; horses/transport can suffer injury; hunting/crafting consumes ammunition/materials; Gambler tables require stakes and may cause losses; time trials can be interrupted. No numeric expenditure/reward/progression is entered into campaign records here. There is no blanket claim that consumed materials are recoverable or that arbitrary violent acts are necessary in Chapter 2. D13 governs Honor/law risk, D12 resource recipes, D06 inventory.

## 4. Story, regional, edition and one-way availability cautions [T0/T1; T2 segregated]

**T0/T1 safe now:** The broad challenge structure is not generally classified as permanently missable for official 100% because post-story cleanup exists. But many **individual stages are temporarily unavailable** pending tutorial, tool, recipe, location or vendor access; the distinction is important. Story Mode gameplay does not inherit Online's challenge/role mechanics. On PC vs console always rely on on-screen control prompts rather than fixed button assumptions. [R01][R03]

**T2 — later access context (do not surface in default Chapter 2 suggestion):**
- **Herbalist 9:** the complete base-game species set includes **43 plants**, with New Austin species blocked by standard early-story access; ordinary, exploit-free progression defers finishing all species until permitted later. Orchids may have quest-related spawn caps, requiring careful inventory/source review rather than a claimed permanent loss. [R06][D12]
- **Horseman 9:** Van Horn → Blackwater has later-region hostility/access risk; guides disagree on whether completing it before the epilogue is practically possible under normal gameplay. Preservation-first advice is to defer until legitimately accessible, with no exploits or required early route. [R01][R08]
- **Bandit 6:** Clemens Cove horse fence becomes available in later main-story progression; Bandit 8's Emerald Ranch wagon fence is a separate service and earlier gate. Check actual fence menu; theft does not prove sale. [R03][D13]
- **Survivalist:** fishing rod normally from Chapter 2 `A Fisher of Men`; acquiring a rod prematurely is not proof Survivalist 1 activated. Do not instruct stealing a rod to bypass progression. [R07]
- **Master Hunter 10:** legendary Giaguaro panther availability tied to completing Master Hunter 1–9; treat its appearance as gated, not a general free-roam spawn. [R01][R09]
- **Bandit / gambling / recipes:** actual poker/blackjack/Five Finger Fillet venues, a horse fence, learned volatile recipe, long scope and specialty ammo might be stage or service dependent; do not assume chapter gate uniform across every platform. [R03][R04][D12]

**Disputed “one-way” possibilities:** guide claims that any of these nine challenge strands is forever missable conflict with generally accepted post-story cleanup. However **one-time treasure-map pickups**, orchid spawn gating and optional full Compendium item completion deserve separate source-level checks; avoid conflating them with 90-rank official 100%. No claim that all 90 can be completed in Chapter 2 or by one character without later access. No outlaw farming exploit recipes.

## 6. Exact 100% scope and rank-by-rank reference [T0/T1 objectives, T2 notes above]

**OFFICIAL_100:** The game's **Player → Challenges** condition requires **all nine strands finished**, each comprising ten sequential ranks = **90 ranked tasks**. The same Player group separately requests maximum Health/Stamina/Dead Eye and horse bonding level 4. `Legend of the East` gear/outfit, trophy achievements and personally exhaustive Compendium gathering are **distinct checks**, even when progression overlaps. View **Pause → Progress → Challenges** for active ranks, and **Pause → Progress → Total Completion → Player** for official category checkmarks. [R01][R03][R12][D15]

**Exactness convention:** These are **source-backed, tightly normalized objective descriptions preserving required quantities/qualifiers**, *not* a claim of direct in-game string transcription. Differences/typos are flagged in §9. The current saved-game objective and counter override any guide shorthand. The reward column documents guide-reported unlocks or cash/XP, not a recorded purchase/payout. Sources for each entire ten-rank strand follow its heading and are itemized §8.

## 7. Three common mistakes and three Chapter 2 field scenarios [T0/T1]

**Mistake A — passive retroactivity:** “I've already gathered all those plants, so Herbalist 9 is done.” **Correction:** rank 9 must be unlocked; pick each qualifying species under that active rank and verify 43-species tracker. A Compendium entry is a separate observation, not rank-specific proof. [R01][D12]

**Mistake B — gear credit:** “I completed all ten Horseman ranks, so Health must be maxed.” **Correction:** a category clear can unlock gear, but level-cap progression depends on required reinforced equipment acquisition/purchase and XP. Verify Trapper receipts and Player stats separately. [R02][R11]

**Mistake C — wrong action/target:** “Any weapon gets Weapons Expert 8; any water fish qualifies for Survivalist 1.” **Correction:** stage 8 requires a long-barreled **sidearm**, and stage 1 specifies **Bluegill** (three in stronger cross-sources). Verify the actual objective text/eligible target. [R01][R07][R10]

**Scenario 1 — optional wildlife practice by Horseshoe Overlook:** If **Master Hunter 1** is showing and a safe, legal deer is found, the player may hunt and **skin** an actual deer, then read the updated 0–3/3 rank count. Killing/studying alone cannot be substituted for the `skin` verb; no assumption that the current rank is active or that skinning has happened. If other player goals currently take priority, leave the hunt for later. [R01][D15]

**Scenario 2 — quiet herb walk:** If **Herbalist 1** is active and the player wants to explore, choose a few nearby pickable **Yarrow**, inspect name before picking, then check the active six-pick meter. A plant seen or already in the Compendium is not sufficient proof. Do not promise 43 plants, later-region availability, or current owned ingredients. [R06][D12]

**Scenario 3 — High Honor + gambler introduction:** If **Gambler 1** is actually available at a poker table, a voluntary low-stakes session may try for five **winning hands**, but playing five hands or winning a whole session by itself is not a substitute. If player prefers no gambling now, offer Horseman 1's safe on-horse wildlife trial only if the active rank and target conditions are confirmed. Avoid automatically steering a High Honor player into Bandit crime. [R01][R04][D13]
