## 1. Definitions, interaction, and in-game feedback [T0/T1]

The **gang-camp donation/tithing box** near Dutch's lodging accepts Arthur's cash and eligible valuables; the money becomes a *shared camp operating fund*. The adjacent **Ledger** records contributions and has pages to buy or restock camp facilities **after its Chapter 2 gate**. Arthur's personal **wallet is a distinct balance**. The story's larger gang robbery/escape stash should **not** be equated to the spendable camp Ledger balance or credited from a mission payment without an actual box balance check. Sources: [E01], [E03], [E05], [E11].

**Read, then act:** At the camp box, inspect the `Contribute` or equivalent displayed prompt (platform button and wording vary). At an unlocked Ledger, inspect balance(s), page labels, cost, `Upgrade` or `Restock` prompt, and confirmation *before* purchasing; subsequent page/physical facility changes are stronger evidence of a purchase than a plan to buy. A locked/missing Ledger is **not evidence of the player being poor**. Sources: [E01–E03].

**Three stocks, not three currencies:** Strauss's medical supplies, Arthur's ammunition wagon, and Pearson's provisions wagon may be collected from in camp. Their **stock icons** are separate from camp cash: **red = low**, **white = usable/mid**, **gold/yellow = well stocked**. The Ledger's `Restock` replenishes stock; a *tier upgrade* changes the varieties available (and the refill price). Donations/stock pickups may influence visible status, but a red icon alone is not evidence that upgrades failed or that cash is negative. Check HUD and menu together. Sources: [E04], [E06], [E07].

**Pearson has two operational purposes:** A donation of eligible meat/carcass for camp **provisions** supports feeding, while **Donate for Crafting** of specified materials reserves ingredients for his satchel/decorative crafting menu. Provision donation is **not** proof a recipe ingredient was banked. Ledger-purchased **Leather Working Tools** enables a wider crafting set; Pearson still needs specific materials and recipe-level conditions. Trapper stock is independent (D03 owns detailed routing). Sources: [E04], [E08], [E14].

**Camp chores** (e.g., wood, hay, water, sacks when prompts appear) are ordinary optional work, different from cash donations, Ledger purchases, or Pearson material hand-ins. Sources: [E09], [E10].

### 3B. Restock is *not* an upgrade (separate recurring fee)

Representative **base full-refill costs at purchased tiers** [E06][E07]:

| Wagon | Default refill | After 1st tier | After 2nd tier | After 3rd tier |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Medicine / Strauss | $15 | $25 | $45 | $75 |
| Provisions / Pearson | $15 | $25 | $40 | $50 |
| Ammunition / Arthur | $8 | $10 | $15 | $20 |

This is **what it costs to resupply**, not a charge for taking a single item; the menu may hide/disable refill when full or vary its displayed action. Actual recipient: gang supply wagon. Refresh one stock if needed; don't grind perpetual yellow icons as a progress requirement. A fresh tier purchase and a later stock refill are different actions.

### 3C. Spend path / money truth table

| Input/action | Destination or payment | Result/proof | Misinterpretation to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Donate Arthur's cash/valuables into tithing box | Shared camp operating fund | Box/ledger contribution and balance when visible [E03][E05] | Not a purchase, not an Arthur wallet reserve; donating everything is optional |
| Buy named Ledger upgrade | Ledger debits camp resources; **wallet shortfall treatment disputed in older guides** | Confirm receipt/ledger status and BOTH balances [E03][E11][E17] | Do not tell player funds must first be transferred *or* that Arthur's wallet always automatically pays without inspecting actual screen |
| Restock wagon | One stock's paid replenishment | Wagon/icon changes after refill [E06][E07] | Upgrade category/tier isn't magically advanced |
| Donate **for provisions** to Pearson | Camp food/provision flow | Game prompt/food feedback [E08][E14] | Does not establish `Donate for Crafting` receipt |
| Donate **for crafting** to Pearson | Pearson recipe input pool | Pearson menu ingredient reflects contribution [E08][E14] | Does not grant tools or satchel automatically |
| Carry out an available chore | Chore performance | Prompt, possible Honor / Dead Eye XP feedback [E09] | Not equivalent to cash deposit or Ledger purchase |

**Disputed payment accounting, consequential:** Published [E01]/[E03]/[E05] say contribute to shared funds before purchase. Newer [E11] explicitly reports Ledger consumes gang funds first, then pulls any shortfall from Arthur's personal wallet, consistent with player purchase reports [E17]. Those can partly coexist as “camp pays first but wallet may cover gap,” but older guides' **mandatory pre-transfer instruction is not universally demonstrated**. **Safe operational rule:** Look at the screen's gang and Arthur balances *before* confirming any upgrade, observe the exact charge/confirmation, and never commit the player's cash to a fictional fund total. Flag wallet-auto-cover as **DISPUTED pending menu-specific reproduction**, not a reason to block U03/U04 research.

## 4. Story/chapter/time/edition risks [T0/T1; later notes isolated]

- **No Chapter 2 automatic unlock:** Ledger follows U02; the box itself may exist before the Ledger. First debt return/deposit is the important event, *not* completing Strauss's entire storyline. [E01][E12]
- **No forced spending/grinding:** An upgrade can be bought later while normal gang-camp access exists; waiting preserves Arthur's money and optional pacing. Do **not** force full satchel crafting, unlock a mission merely for a convenience upgrade, or advance chapters without an explicit player choice.
- **Consumable allocation:** Ordinary animal materials and valuables may be irreversibly routed to donation, Pearson, butcher or Trapper. Before submitting a scarce perfect pelt, inspect the real recipe and donation prompt; D03/D04 own precise material effects. [E08][E14]
- **Camp stocks deplete:** Yellow is a supply level, not permanent ownership of items. Tier unlocks improve future options, not infinite supply. [E04][E07]
- **Honor feedback:** Community guides associate chores and suitably sized camp contributions with positive Honor. Precise Honor points/threshold and whether every contribution type has equal effect are **not** guaranteed by sources here; use in-game Honor feedback, avoid claiming a fixed farmable reward. [E09][E16]
- **Achievements are a separate chapter-window obligation:** If the player targets `Give to the Poor`, **cumulative $250 personally contributed to the gang tithing box during Chapters 2–4**, before the donation box disappears, is described as missable; it is **not** official Total Completion. Do not promise an arbitrary Ledger upgrade or an ordinary gang-member donation counts toward *Arthur's personal qualifying contribution* without direct trophy evidence. [E18][E19]
- **[T2, opt-in future-location detail]** Boat availability is later camp/location-dependent (commonly Chapter 3/4), not a purchase at Horseshoe Overlook. Listed boat price varies $450/$360 in sources; verify edition and actual menu. The gang-camp donation box/upgrade purchase window does not persist for the full story. [E04][E05][E19]

**Player observation overrides generalized availability** for this playthrough, but one player's screenshot does not make a universal rule. A source date does not prove identical prices in every edition/platform.

## 7. Three common mistakes + three spoiler-light conditional examples [T0/T1]

**Mistakes to avoid:**
1. **“Strauss chain complete = required to buy a camp upgrade.”** Wrong normal hard gate: recover **one** opening debt and **deposit** it. Verify box/ledger prompt. [E01][E12]
2. **“I bought the Ledger, so Pearson can craft my new satchel.”** Ledger is unlocked, **not purchased**; **Tools are a separate $225-base purchase**; each crafted item still has its own required inputs. [E03][E08]
3. **“Red camp icon / $0 camp cash means Arthur has no money or my wallet is automatically donated.”** Three icons indicate wagon stock; gang fund and wallet are distinct; check purchase confirmation and balances. [E07][E11]

**Scenario A — first camp tour, no visible Ledger:** If Arthur sees the box but no usable Ledger and a Strauss debt task actually appears, the companion says: “The normal Ledger gate is *one collected debt brought back and deposited*. Do you want to pursue that optional camp feature, or just enjoy camp/ride out?” It does **not** declare the quest completed, require the full chain, or request a screenshot of unrelated inventory.

**Scenario B — Ledger opens, player wants better inventory:** Inspect actual `Leather Working Tools` entry, current menu price and both money balances. If player chooses it, purchase through Ledger and **confirm success**; **then** inspect one Pearson recipe before a modest hunting outing. A red provisions indicator or generic meat donation does not prove a crafting ingredient. Do not promise any satchel is owned.

**Scenario C — player wants faster rides, not a completion grind:** With U02 observed open, present **Dutch $220 -> Arthur $325** as one optional **$545 base** path. Inspect actual edition prices and available cash; if purchased, verify the map at Arthur's area. On a current patched Story Mode build, mention wilderness-camp outbound fast travel too. If cash is tight, ordinary horseback travel and later purchase remain valid. [E02][E13]
