## 1. Definitions and player interaction, including visible feedback [T0/T1]

A **Legendary Animal** in Story Mode is a one-of-a-kind, designated wildlife encounter, normally tracked by exploring its territory and sequentially inspecting signs. There are **16 legendary animals** in the standard Story Mode guide catalog [E02]; do not confuse these with Red Dead Online's much larger Naturalist animal collection [E14]. A legendary animal is **not** an ordinary three-star animal whose flawless hide requires a clean weapon match: the **unique legendary skin/pelt is not degraded by weapon choice or multiple damaging shots**, unlike D04 regular hunting. Prioritize a safe, effective shot and player survival [E02,E04]. Do not recommend gratuitous ammunition expenditure as a requirement.

**Normal hunt UI sequence (contextual platform controls):**
1. In normal free roam after the relevant introduction, inspect **Satchel → Documents → Maps → Legendary Animal Map** if actually present. The map is an *approximate territory clue*, not a guarantee the animal is standing at the drawing [E02].
2. Ride/walk into a plausible territory until a top-left **“entered Legendary Animal Territory”** message (or equivalent) appears. The crowned animal drawing on the world map can help, but **not seeing the icon yet** does not prove the animal was killed or unavailable [E02,E10].
3. Turn on **Eagle Eye** (on standard controller configurations, both sticks; keyboard/remapped input varies) and look for golden/yellow clue particles or an interaction question mark. Inspect the first sign (droppings, fur, tracks), follow the indicated route, inspect further signs, then follow the final trail. Guides describe **usually around three signs**, *not* a guaranteed fixed number or fixed coordinate [E02,E05,E10].
4. Distinguish **`TRACKED`**, **`SEEN/STUDIED`**, **`KILLED`**, **`SKINNED`** and **`ITEM RECEIVED`** on the actual Compendium/notifications. Their exact flags differ; don't infer every one from the existence of the map icon [E02,D15]. For risk-sensitive observations, use the live prompt and Compendium/Total Completion readback.
5. After an actual kill, if the carcass is accessible, **skin it where appropriate**, verify named **Legendary [Animal] Pelt/Hide/Skin** and any separately named trophy part (e.g. Buck Antler/Bear Claw) in the satchel, and choose destination **by recipe**. For certain animals/carass types, selling an intact carcass to the Trapper may also be available [E01,E04,E05].

**Do not confuse** `Eagle Eye` trail highlighting with `Dead Eye` targeting or a guaranteed ability stage (D09 owns Dead Eye); a mounted trail, a corpse icon and a Compendium check are different observations. The map or a gold particle effect is not itself proof the specific animal spawned, was killed or registered.

## 3. Benefits, costs, destination routing and recoverability

### 3A. Four separate outcomes of a successful unique hunt

| Event/result | Destination and benefit | What is NOT implied |
|---|---|---|
| **Legendary kill** | May advance separate **5 Legendary Animals Killed** Total Completion threshold when registered; one-time species kill [E02,E08,E09] | Not skinning, unique trophy part ownership, Fence item, Trapper outfit, or `Zoologist`. |
| **Legendary pelt / hide / eligible carcass** | Physically deliver/sell to **Trapper** for sale revenue **and** usable legendary garment material; material can later enable multiple named outfit pieces [E01,E04,E13] | Not automatic clothing purchase or guaranteed money if physical delivery was skipped. Ordinary perfect pelts are **not** covered by Rockstar loss-insurance rule. |
| **Separately named trophy component (antler/claw/tooth/horn/heart/etc.)** | Hold in Arthur's **Satchel** for named **Fence → Craft** trinket or talisman [E03,E06,D06] | Not a standard Trapper hide; never advise selling the component as a common valuable. No first-party evidence the Rockstar Trapper recovery explicitly banks this separately for a Fence. |
| **Crafted gear/perk** | Trapper requires recipe ingredient(s) + fee to acquire **clothes/selected gear**; Fence requires special part + possibly other components/fee to acquire **trinket/talisman** [E03,E04] | Legendary pelt banking ≠ per-item purchase; owning a perk ≠ having caught fish or completed official 100%. |

**First-party loss rule, narrow and explicit:** Rockstar Support [E01] states that *after a Legendary Animal is killed*, if Arthur **cannot skin it** or loses the **pelt/carcass before reaching a Trapper**, those items become available **in the Trapper's inventory after a short delay** for use at those shops. The support text **does not say** the player receives the cash they would have earned for personally selling the pelt, retrieves the physical pelt to the horse/satchel, automatically owns a coat, receives a duplicate carcass or automatically has an independently required **Fence** item. Do not extend this rule to ordinary three-star pelts or all held rare collectibles.

**Revenue vs entitlement:** Carrying/selling the intact legendary pelt to a Trapper normally creates **sale money** at the moment of sale, then Trapper **crafting eligibility** for items subject to other recipe requirements. Losing the pelt can still preserve *Trapper recipe material entitlement* under Rockstar's rule; secondary guides/player evidence indicate the **unrealized sale revenue is forgone**, but Rockstar's precise statement does **not** explicitly quantify this [E01,E13]. Safe wording: “Your Trapper **crafting material** can be recovered; do **not expect the sale payout** unless you physically sell it.”

**One legendary animal / multiple garment references:** Trapper item lists can show the same legendary bear/buck material on several recipes, even though each specimen is unique. Multiple independent firsthand reports indicate the once-delivered legendary material can satisfy all relevant named legendary garment uses (with other materials and fees still required) [E04,E13]. This is **secondary/community evidence**, not a promise that every missing regular ingredient is duplicated; inspect the Trapper per-recipe requirement and marked availability. **Ordinary perfect pelts remain consumable quantity requirements** per D04/D06 and are NOT multiplied the same way.

## 4. Story/chapter/time/edition risks and disputes

- **One-off kill / preservation:** Treat each unique encounter as a **single chance to observe, optionally study, then skin/collect its separate component**. After death, animal normally does not respawn; Rockstar protects Trapper **pelt/carcass materials** under its stated conditions, **not a comprehensive complete-Compendium replay**. If doing personally exhaustive tracking/study/skinning, observe the verbs before/after the one-time encounter [E01,E02,D15].
- **Hunters in Chapter 2:** Hosea's introductory bear provides the map but leaving during the choice does not destroy the hunt; Chapter 2 is **not an order to clear 16 legends immediately** [E02].
- **Interrupted pursuit / territory “busy”:** The phrase “too much activity” is a contextual local blocker, not verified as “wrong hour,” “must kill NPCs” or precise cooldown; leave and later return. If repeatedly absent without a prompt, check story gate and area first. Avoid needless resets that endanger a live save [E02,E10,E11].
- **Weapon quality exception:** Legendary pelt is not downgraded by extra weapon damage; ordinary perfect pelts **are** subject to three-star/clean kill and weapon appropriateness. The Buck Trinket enhancement does not make ordinary hunting automatically perfect [E02,E06,E12,D04].
- **Bounty/predator hazard:** Hunt safely, avoid assaulting bystanders or assuming a hunter can pause mission Gold timers. Legendary prey can fight or flee, horses can die, and harvested pelt can be lost on death/cargo incident; D01 governs ordinary cargo and mount lifecycle [D01,D04].
- **Edition/catalog:** Special/Ultimate Edition extra trinkets and the December 2019 Story Mode content update add items **not all tied to the 16 base legendary hunts**; neither online Naturalist legendary lists nor a special-edition item's perk should be interpreted as required to get five official legendary kills [E03,E14,D15].
- **[T2 OPT-IN]** Some legendary animals require later region or story access, e.g. Bullgator, New Austin animals, and challenge rank 9 Panther. Neither crossing out an unreachable map illustration nor a generic walkthrough location proves current Chapter 2 unlock. Never prescribe chapter skip or exploit for this [E02,E03,E08].
- **Skin Deep / Zoologist boundary:** Specialist guide [E02,E12] says Legendary animals are **not required** for these separate achievements, because they are one-off and potentially unavailable to skin/study. This is a **secondary trophy-guide classification**; the **current user's achievement status** must be observed on platform. It does not erase the five-kills `Best in the West` category.
- **Sale-versus-recovery distinction:** The first-party lost-pelt article promises Trapper usage after delay, **not** a refund of hypothetical merchant sale revenue. Reports that all legendary special Fence parts magically respawn are beyond Rockstar's documented text; leave Fence material recovery **UNKNOWN** unless precisely observed.

## 7. Three common mistakes and three spoiler-light conditional Chapter 2 examples

**Common mistakes**
1. “Legendary hides work like an ordinary 3★ deer and become poor if I take multiple shots.” **No**—normal perfect-pelt quality mechanics do not govern the unique legendary pelt. Safety/weapon power matter, not protecting an ordinary three-star grade [E02,D04].
2. “I lost the legendary pelt, so I lost all its possible crafts and must find another legendary animal.” **Too broad**—Rockstar protects the lost **Trapper material** after a short delay, but **not guaranteed sale cash or all Fence parts**. Confirm Trapper inventory; don't assume animal respawn [E01].
3. “One legend killed means a Trapper coat and a Fence trinket are mine, and one animal is enough for the 100% row.” **No**—sell/automatic material access, buy craft, held Fence part, and **five kills** are distinct proofs [E03,E04,E08].

**Example A — Hosea outing:** In Chapter 2, **if** `Exit Pursued by a Bruised Ego` is actually offered/played, the companion explains the legendary bear tutorial and the optional **leave or continue** choice. If player leaves, say “You may return to the bear later when ready.” Before recommending another legend, check that the map is actually present; do not declare the bear killed, pelt owned or one of five recorded. If player is in a Gold-timed replay, defer completely to the full mission guide [E02,D01].

**Example B — short wildlife recon with Buck, only after introduction:** **If** the player confirms Legendary Map and suitable free-roam conditions, offer one optional Legendary Buck clue-following experience (not a promise of a spawn). When Eagle Eye clues appear, inspect several signs and take safe aim if the animal is actually present. If killed and skinned, **verify the Buck Antler separately** from the legendary pelt; later consider the Trapper and Fence only if each menu actually offers the required service. Mention Buck Antler perk as a possible convenience, not automatic perfect ordinary hides. Do not impose five legendary hunts or an equipment shopping list [E03,E06,D04,D06].

**Example C — lost load, calm recovery:** Player reports a confirmed **killed** legendary animal, then a lost pelt on a horse before reaching a Trapper. Answer: “Rockstar says that legendary pelt/carcass material will appear in the Trapper inventory **after a short delay**. Check his appropriate garment recipe after time has passed. The missed sale money isn't confirmed recoverable; the Fence component needs its own inventory/Craft check. Ordinary pelts don't get this guarantee.” If player says only **‘it ran away’**, do **not** invoke recovery; troubleshoot territory/track later. No fabricated loss, pickup, autosave or regained funds [E01,E10,D01].
