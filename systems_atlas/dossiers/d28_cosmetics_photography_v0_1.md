## 3. Benefits, costs, item routing, recoverability and risks

**Mostly aesthetic:** hairstyle, facial hair, clothing colors, hats, pomade, self-portrait pose, camera angle/filter, postcards/pictures, newsprint and repeat performances serve immersion/role-play. NPCs may comment on poor grooming or mud; sources do **not** establish a reliable universal Honor increase, wanted-level reset or core restoration simply from a barber/bath [C06,C09,C10].

**Gameplay-relevant distinctions:**
- D10 establishes **outfit climate suitability impacts Health Core drain**; not every good-looking suit is weather-suitable. Appearance color is not a thermal rating. D13/D10 establish face coverings do **not** guarantee lawman anonymity [C09].
- Acquired talismans/trinkets can add **passive mechanical bonuses** even when no talisman is visibly worn; ordinary fashion pieces do not automatically do so [C11,C12].
- Actual **Bath, Show and Theater** qualify for three official Miscellaneous **subset rows** [C13–C15].
- A named quest's handheld Camera requirement can progress a **Stranger stage**; scenic photos by themselves do not [C16,C17].

**Payment:** Barber, bath, clothing, show and theater have optional costs at menus. Published 2018 examples: ordinary Valentine bath **$0.25**, Valentine show **$0.50**, live Saint Denis theater **$2**; these are *historical reference examples*, **not** claims about current screen price, owned cash, discounts or purchased tickets [C13–C15]. Photo Mode has no documented Story Mode cash fee. No in-game stat increase is claimed from spending more on deluxe bath.

**Reversibility:** Short hair must **grow** back; barber cannot instantly add length. Tonic can accelerate growth; exact formula, maximum stages and chapter restrictions are disputed [C07,C08]. Fashion choice can ordinarily be changed later if items are owned, but purchasing accessories still spends money. Image editing is reversible before capture; photo saved in an in-game gallery should not be treated as an externally backed-up picture [C05,C20].

**Risk/privacy:** Save Photo may store in platform-linked photo gallery; publishing image online is **optional** and should never be initiated without the player. Image may reveal in-game spoilers/locations. Historical photo capacity reports disagree (96 versus 200), and no current 2026 service/device limit was verified [C20]. Do not delete photos to test a guessed limit or equate an in-game photograph with manual **Save Game**. For active Gold or one-time loot, GameGuide mission warnings take priority over recreational photos.

## 5. Photo mode versus Camera: practical controls, capture, editing and saving

### Handheld Camera (in-world / task-sensitive)
1. Enter **Item Wheel** by the usual weapon wheel access and switch to **Items**. In guide examples, Camera shares/cycles from the Binoculars slot. If Camera does not appear, ask what is currently visible rather than claiming ownership [C02,C16].
2. Raise Camera/viewfinder as the **on-screen** context permits. Standard controller quest walkthrough uses **L2/LT** aim and **R2/RT** to take photo; PC mappings/remaps differ [C16].
3. In casual camera context, a self-portrait/posed view may offer expression/pose cycling; inspect current prompts and save image via UI [C02].
4. For a named mission demanding a particular subject photograph, make the shot **through this physical tool**, then verify the task text or stage changes. It is NOT established that an image from Pause Photo Mode or OS screenshot triggers quest progression [C16].
5. Check game photo gallery; use a separate screenshot/export if user wants a durable copy. **Do not claim** the picture is in a known Windows picture folder without verifying actual storage [C05,C20].

### Pause-menu Photo Mode (scene composition / not a physical item)
1. In patched Story Mode **Pause → Photo Mode**, or **F6** on original default PC keyboard if active. Device/controller-specific shortcut is context-dependent; don't invent button mappings [C01,C04].
2. Switch **Orbit Camera** (centres on player) and **Free Camera** (reposition within a limited range). PC Gamer reports V to toggle, H to hide HUD, Q/E roll, Tab option categories; always follow displayed help/remaps [C04].
3. Choose lens, field of view/zoom, focus distance, depth-of-field blur, exposure, camera rotation/roll and filters/contrast/intensity where available. Add **stickers or text** through relevant editor if desired. Rockstar's official 2019 release explicitly mentions filters, stickers and text [C03,C04].
4. Invoke **Save Photo** in game when composition is satisfactory; inspect **View Photos** / gallery. For a personally controlled standalone high-quality file, one can use a platform or GPU screenshot after hiding HUD. PC Gamer notes the game Social Club photo output can be compressed compared with an external capture [C04,C05].
5. Upload/share only with explicit player decision and actual connected platform-service support. The historical Rockstar name was **Social Club**; current account/upload behavior must be verified, not assumed [C05,C20].

**Function differences:** Hand Camera exists as an in-world prop and is required by some script objectives. Photo Mode is a visual composition/editor controlled through a game menu and does **not** move Arthur's body, purchase items, study an animal for Compendium, register POI, complete a photo quest by default, or automatically save the campaign. Photo Mode availability mid-cutscene/mission is **context dependent/untested**; do not promise free use in every mission or an exploit of Gold timing [C16,C19,C23].

**Gallery size dispute:** historical anecdotes claim **200 PC vs 96 consoles**, but conflicting posts show no official universal published limit. Do not encode these numbers as a hard capacity/cleanup policy; follow visible capacity and safe exports [C20].

## 8. Three common errors and three organic Chapter 2 T0/T1 examples

**Error A — category conflation:** a 2018 guide calls the handheld camera a 'photo mode' even though the fully free Photo Mode came with 2019 PC/console updates. **Safe:** ask 'Item Wheel Camera or Pause Photo Mode?' [C01–C03].  
**Error B — universal beauty checklist:** every haircut, bath variant, Photo Mode image and performance is treated as official 100%. **Safe:** exactly one Bath, Show and Theater in separate Total Completion rows; photos themselves optional [C13–C17].  
**Error C — Story/Online or photo storage leakage:** Online-exclusive bonus outfits, Modern role cameras, 96/200 hard gallery count and gallery upload are assumed universally. **Safe:** mode/edition/patch and actual menu first [C03,C20,C21].

### Scenario 1 — Valentine grooming with optional 100% bath (T0)

If player freely visits Valentine in Chapter 2 and accessible hotel offers a Bath, pay **displayed** price and take ordinary bath; optionally choose deluxe for immersion, not extra official credit. Verify **Pause → Progress → Total Completion → Bath**, not number of times washed. If barber chair available, preview haircut and beard style based on **currently grown** length; shaving is reversible only with time, and not a second 100% item [C06,C07,C10,C13]. No assumption player paid, possesses Hair Tonic, is currently filthy or has a checkmark.

### Scenario 2 — early evening moving-picture Show (T0/T1)

If Valentine's ticketed magic-lantern show is offered and no **player-confirmed active time-limited mission** needs attention, watch one full eligible show and then inspect the **Show** checkbox. It is not proof of the **Theater** checkbox; published sources assign that to a separate live theater in another town. Attending many repeats is optional; current venue schedule and exact ticket price require UI verification [C14,C15].

### Scenario 3 — camp-landscape photo outing versus a named request (T0/T1)

If Pause Photo Mode exists, use Orbit/Free camera, lens/filter, Save Photo and verify Gallery—or take a user-controlled system screenshot for a durable personal copy. **No official 100% photo target**. If a named Stranger later **explicitly** requests a photograph of a subject, instead use an available **physical Camera** via Item Wheel, act according to current quest prompts, and check task progression; it is a distinct staged objective. Photo Mode is not an established substitute [C02,C04,C05,C16,C17]. Do not assert the named quest has started or disclose later quest outcomes.
