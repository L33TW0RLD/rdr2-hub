## 0. Playable, spoiler-light default

**The item's exact in-game name + visible description + intended recipient matter more than the fact it appears under “Valuables”.** A sale button is not a promise that selling preserves crafting, collectible or companion-request utility. [C01, C05, C07, C09, C14]

1. **INSPECT** the object in Satchel → its category, description, visible count and capacity, then check any relevant recipe/request/Compendium or treasure clue. Do not infer an item from an icon alone.
2. **PROTECT** one-off-looking documents, named tokens, mission/request objects, rare rewards and unidentified unique valuables until checked. “Unique” is a risk cue, **not** proof of absolute non-disposability. [C11, C15]
3. **RESERVE** one verified example of each potentially talisman-relevant jewelry piece — Gold Jointed Bracelet, Silver Chain Bracelet, Gold Earring, Silver Earring — while the relevant Fence recipe is outstanding. This is a practical optional-completion policy, not evidence these items are already owned. [C06, C07]
4. **DO NOT SELL ingredients to a Fence first** when intending to craft a talisman there. Select the **Craft** service with materials still in the player's inventory; selling the item to the Fence is different from handing materials to the Trapper for Trapper recipes. [C07, C08]
5. **SELL** a verified ordinary, surplus valuable (e.g. extra standard ring or gold bar from a completed treasure) only after ruling out a request/craft/collection objective, and after verifying that a general Fence can transact. **DONATE** only deliberately to the correct camp channel, not as a surrogate for vendor crafting. [C03, C04, C09, C12]
6. **STORE** is not one universal command. Satchel items do not go into a separate general-purpose horse chest. Horse physical pelts/carcasses, outfit/hat loadouts, and the patched Story Mode **weapons locker** are different facilities. [C02, C10, C18]
7. **CHECK ACTUAL CAPACITY** from the item limit shown in the current satchel. Pearson satchels expand categories, the Legend of the East satchel expands most stack limits substantially, and notable exceptions (such as watches) exist. An acquired item cap does **not** prove every item has identical limits. [C02, C19]

Classification verbs used here are *advisor* states, not real game commands: **INSPECT**, **HOLD/VERIFY**, **PROTECT**, **RESERVE**, **USE**, **CRAFT**, **SELL**, **DONATE**, **STORE-ONLY-IF-REAL-FACILITY**. Multiple may be valid at different times.

## 2. Inventory ownership model and capacity (S17.M01)

**A. Personal Satchel.** Gameplay categorizes provisions, tonics, ingredients, materials, kit, valuables and documents. Limits are **item and category specific**: an ordinary item might show 3/3, 5/5, 10/10 etc; many unique documents are effectively individual entries. The Red Dead Wiki's granular cap table is community-maintained; check the current game's count before publishing a universal per-item cap. [C02, C19]

**B. Crafted satchels.** Pearson's specialty satchels expand distinct categories (Valuables, Materials, Provisions, Kit, Ingredients, Tonics); the Legend of the East version substantially raises capacity, commonly **up to 99 for most items**, not all. The routes involve a conditional Ledger → Leather Working Tools purchase → craft prerequisites; no ownership is presumed. **D03 owns exact recipe costs and unlock matrix**, so this dossier must not be used to ratify material requirements. [C02, C19]

**C. Horse transport / loadout.** Certain hunting pelts and carcasses are physically placed on the mount (D01/D04); long guns and horse outfits/hats are managed in specialized interfaces. A saddlebag “empty” indicator does **not** expose a transferable overflow stash for watches or herbs. For newer Story Mode builds, **Title Update 1.20** added a weapons locker accessible in player camp from Chapter 2; it stores *unused weapons* rather than arbitrary items. [C10, C18] Platform/patch should be checked if no locker appears.

**D. Recipient inventories.** Pearson's crafting donations and Trapper sales establish destination-specific material access. A Fence's general **SELL** interface is an outright exchange, while talisman **CRAFT** consumes qualifying pieces from current inventory; no shared item bank exists across Fence/Pearson/Trapper/butcher. [C07, C08, C09]

**E. Ammo is a separate capacity regime.** Weapon-class-specific carried-ammo counters and equipment upgrades must be inspected in the gunsmith and weapon wheel. A Legend of the East satchel is **not** an “all ammunition 99” rule. [C21]

**Sensible full-cap response:** compare exact stack with on-screen limit → choose consume/craft/legitimate recipient sale if suitable → consider a relevant Pearson satchel if gate and materials are truly available → avoid discarding a unique item. Don't promise a depot or transferable horse stash.

## 5. Limits, loss and irreversibility audit

- **High-risk, potentially irreversible:** selling/donating the last copy of needed talisman jewelry; handing the wrong named pen/request gift to a cash sink; leaving a one-off location without observing a quest-linked collectible; using an exclusive ingredient without checking target recipe; missing a transient chapter request. Conservative safe default = **HOLD/VERIFY**, not invented reload solution. [C07, C14, C15]
- **Recoverable with a specific official exception:** losing **legendary pelt/carcass** before Trapper delivery — Rockstar says their Trapper materials arrive after short delay; **do not generalize** to money, intact normal carcasses or arbitrary special items. [C16]
- **Conditional and often replaceable:** generic jewelry, cards via Premium Cigarette Packs and ordinary valuables can sometimes be sourced again, but this does not guarantee immediate replacement or make selling the *last needed copy* harmless. [C05, C08, C17]
- **Storage misconception:** no supported indefinite “overflow” via horse saddlebag for satchel valuables; only proven purpose-built systems. [C10, C18]
- **Loss upon death/arrest, horse death, mission replay, story chapter transfer:** exact item-specific deletion/persistence rules **NOT VERIFIED**. This dossier does not guarantee general inventory survival or recommend exploiting save/reload. Ask GameGuide to check its active mission/save protocol before any risky irreversible action.
- **Story-versus-Online drift:** ignore Online-specific collector satchels, trader wagon stocks, animal sample storage and Online pamphlets as Story Mode mechanic sources.

- **OFFICIAL_100 / C100.CARDS candidate:** **one complete cigarette-card set** (12 distinct cards) is enough for that particular Total Completion row; this is **not** a claim all 144 cards are required for the row. Completing the wider “Smoking and Other Hobbies” collectible/Stranger quest involves additional sets/rewards. [C17]

## 8. Three spoiler-light decision scenarios, plus common errors

### Scenario A — A Silver Chain Bracelet appears in the Satchel
**Decision:** **RESERVE one copy**, inspect Fence's Bear Claw Talisman requirements and present jewelry stock. If the craft is not available yet, keeping it is valid. If there is a second identical bracelet and no further goal, surplus sale is *conditionally* defensible. **Never sell the needed bracelet to the Fence first in the belief the Fence will remember it.** No ownership claim is being made. [C06–C08]

### Scenario B — A reward from a treasure-map hunt
**Decision:** Open Documents/Maps and assess whether the chain is actually solved. A verified gold bar already obtained from its cache can be sold at a general Fence or deliberately donated; don't call the gold bar a “quest clue,” and don't imply selling completes the Treasure Hunter goal. Inspect actual progress row. [C03, C12, C17]

### Scenario C — A named pen found while camp is active
**Decision:** Compare the **exact name** against the Task Log request: **Fountain Pen** is the known Mary-Beth request object in Chapter 2, not “any pen”. Preserve until hand-in while the eligible camp dialogue is accessible; if the request isn't active, the window/status remains **UNKNOWN**, not auto-marked missed or completed. [C14, C15]

### Common errors to prevent
1. Calling all valuables “junk” or interpreting “Sell” availability as proof no crafting/request use. **Fix:** variant + dependency check.
2. Treating Pearson, Trapper, butcher, Fence and camp box as one pooled craft inventory. **Fix:** verify exact recipient / craft-vs-sale interface.
3. Promising an imaginary warehouse in the saddlebags or a universal “99 of everything” upgrade. **Fix:** inspect item-level cap, distinguish weapon locker and outfit slots.
4. Claiming a lost ordinary pelt or a Quest Item is recoverable because Rockstar protects lost **legendary pelts**. **Fix:** apply narrowly.
5. Equating the cigarette card Compendium entry with a *physically mail-ready set*. **Fix:** check held card quantities and post-office prompt.
