## 1. Mechanics, interfaces and observable feedback — S14.M01–M03 (T0)

**Ordinary fishing** is a repeatable activity at permitted rivers, lakes, ponds and swamps: equip Fishing Rod from item wheel, select **bait or a lure**, cast into accessible water, wait/animate lure, set hook on a strong bite, control tension and reel while fish tires, land, then choose **keep or release** [E01][E02][E03].

| Stage | Player action and available UI evidence | If absent / ambiguous |
|---|---|---|
| **Rod** | Weapon/item wheel displays usable Fishing Rod; can equip while standing in a suitable reachable fishing spot. | UNKNOWN if missing; see U06, not a claim of ownership simply because it is Chapter 2 |
| **Tackle** | Rod/bait wheel shows named bread/corn/cheese, live bait, regular river/lake/swamp lure, or (later) **Special** variant. | Inventory/shop stock must be inspected; rod and lure are separate unlock/ownership facts |
| **Cast** | Aim at accessible water, hold cast to choose range, release. Float/lure appears. | Invalid terrain/shore, current mission or control mode may prevent cast; obey prompt |
| **Attract & hook** | Bobber/live bait: wait for sustained pull (nibbles are not the same); lure: retrieve slowly/occasionally flick. When rod bends or bobber sinks, press platform hook input. | Premature hook can miss; a fish visible underwater does not prove it has bitten |
| **Fight** | During fish's strong struggle use rod direction to resist its travel without aggressive simultaneous reeling; avoid line tension/snap. | Fighting phase ≠ tired phase, so do not blindly reel at maximum speed |
| **Land** | Fish reaches Arthur; game displays name/weight and a decision or inventory routing. | Visual near shore alone is not a confirmed catch; inspect fish and Compendium |
| **Disposition** | Keep (food/satchel/horse depending size) or return to water; verify UI feedback and items/compendium. | Release does not mean mailed; keep does not mean cooked or posted |

**Standard PlayStation controller, illustrative (not required on PC):** hold L1 for wheel, R1 to item wheel, select rod; bait wheel; hold L2 to grip, R2 hold/release cast, R2 press to hook, left stick to guide rod, right stick clockwise to reel; Circle to cancel/cut [E01][E02]. Equivalent Xbox controls use LB/RB/LT/RT and B as indicated on current display. PC mouse/keyboard use separate mapping (often right mouse grip, left mouse cast/hook, Space reel), and may be customized [E04]. **The live contextual prompt takes precedence over these defaults.**

**Official accessibility control (important edition update):** Rockstar Title Update **1.15** added **Settings > Controls > Hold to Reel (Fishing)** for Story Mode; when enabled, the controller stick rotation becomes **hold Square on PlayStation / X on Xbox** [E05]. This is distinct from ordinary Tap Assist. The setting's presence should be **verified** on the installed build. The player never needs to exhaustively spin a physical stick to follow this research when a supported hold control is available.

**Fishing observation/Compendium distinction:** Catching one new species can record that species in **Progress > Compendium > Fish** and advance the independently tracked **Progress > Total Completion > Fish** distinct-species target; opening the entry does not prove mailing a legendary specimen or completing Survivalist rank [E06][R01].

## 3. Ordinary bait, lures and fish behavior (T0)

| Tackle group | Typical Story Mode use | Constraints / cost |
|---|---|---|
| **Bread, corn, cheese (bobber bait)** | Generally attracts **smaller** fish. Example: bread for perch/redfin pickerel, corn for chain pickerel/bullhead, cheese for bluegill/rock bass [E03][E09]. | Passive float; consumes or risks bait on take/loss; vendor price/stock differs across sources — check shop |
| **Worms, crickets, crayfish** | Live bait for medium fish or larger ordinary fish; worms often trout, crickets bass, crayfish deeper-water bass [E03][E04][E09]. | Consumable; species preference is **guidance, not spawn guarantee** |
| **Ordinary lake/river/swamp lures** | Reusable artificial tackle selected by habitat/target medium/large fish; retrieve gently rather than just waiting for float [E01][E04][E09]. | Line break can lose lure; match water type pragmatically, no exact guaranteed fish |
| **Special lake/river/swamp lures** | Legendary fishing after relevant quest/store gate; use **special** lure, **not** food bait; match water for a good practical default [E12][E15]. | Later unlock and usually money cost; guide says special lure required for 13 quest fish, no guarantee of immediate bite |
| **Special Spinner** | Later quest reward, usable widely across habitats [E17][E09]. | **Not an early Chapter 2 starting tool**; later access T2 only |

**Species in ordinary fish catalogue (15 nonlegendary fish) [E09][E18]:** Bluegill, Bullhead Catfish, Chain Pickerel, Perch, Redfin Pickerel, Rock Bass, Largemouth Bass, Smallmouth Bass, Steelhead Trout, Sockeye Salmon, Channel Catfish, Longnose Gar, Lake Sturgeon, Muskie, Northern Pike. **Do not mistake the full 15 ordinary-species list for a required early-game 15-catch quest**, and don't mistake a species' legendary counterpart for a second normal species. Local water, fish presence, weather/time and lure affect actual success.

**Water guidance:** shore access plus visible fish matter more than a rigid one-size-fits-all pin. Fish can be found in freshwater rivers/lakes/ponds/bayous; avoid assuming every species exists near Horseshoe Overlook. Fish size/storage is a separate practical variable: small and many medium fish route to satchel, substantial catches to horse carrying; inspect the action prompt and inventory [E01][E13]. No universal numeric storage limit claimed.

## 4. Catch outcomes, resources, Honor and risks — S14.M03

- **KEEP** ordinary fish for sale, edible fish preparation, camp contributions where accepted, or personal record; exact yield of a given species and cooking seasoning recipes are **D12's domain**. The available fish-size carrying slots and what is currently on the horse are **D01/D06's domain**, not automatic permanent inventory [E03][E04].
- **RELEASE** an ordinary fish via the catch-choice prompt. Red Dead Wiki reports **positive Honor** from release, but there is no demonstrated invariant honor quantity per single fish: treat as **general tendency / MEDIUM**, with actual on-screen Honor indicator/player report governing result [E03]. Release is not a mailed legendary-fish objective.
- **Health growth:** Red Dead Wiki states fish catches can yield Health XP [E03]; no universal exact XP per species is certified here. Other growth/cores belong to D09, and catching a fish once does not prove maximum stat progress.
- **Lost line / lure:** reeling aggressively during active fish struggle can snap line and lose attached tackle [E01][E15]. Ease off while fighting, steer rod, resume when tired; use Hold to Reel if enabled. No deterministic bite wait time.
- **Horse/load and risks:** larger retained fish/legendary cargo may be exposed to death/horse separation/time and must reach relevant recipient; inspect actual cargo and post office, don't promise every fish survives loss. PowerPyx claims certain **lost large legendary fish can be recaught** [E13], but broader sources dispute repeatability/marked-map interpretation; preserve saves and verify the catch/quest counter rather than guarantee a respawn [E13][E19].
- **Story fishing vs Story mission Gold:** A Fisher of Men is also a story mission with its *own* Gold timers [E07]. The tutorial's medal-specific requirements belong in the **full canonical Mission Control Field Guide**, not this generic fishing dossier. Free-roam fishing does not retrospectively award an eligible mission medal.

**Default safety:** if no rod visible → recommend story tutorial or defer, not fictional casting. If tackle unavailable → inspect store/wheel; do not recommend legendary fishing in Ch2. If a one-time legendary catch is observed, prioritize safe transport and mailing, without inventing what was deposited.


## 5. Legendary fishing versus ordinary fishing — S14.M04 and S27.M03

**Different target, different gate, different post-catch duty.** Ordinary fish can be caught with suitable common bait/standard lures once fishing is available. Jeremy Gill's **13 named Legendary Fish** are a later Stranger/Collectibles chain: obtain map after accepting the quest, acquire Special Lures, catch specimens at their special waters and **mail each to Gill** at a post office; do not equate a map illustration, discovered Compendium entry, or catch animation with a confirmed mail hand-in [E12][E13][E14].

**Spoiler-light procedure for after the gate is observed:**

1. Verify **A Fisher of Fish** actually accepted (named Gill mission/map), **Fishing Rod** equipped and at least one **Special** lure actually owned. The guide's normal access window is **Chapter 3 onward**, not first Chapter 2 free roam [E12][E14].
2. Consult the fish map and the **body of water**: lake, river or swamp special variant. A matched type is the strongest practical default. **Some community/reference sources argue any Special Lure can attract any legendary fish**, so don't falsely state every single species is technically impossible with a different Special Lure; exact lure preference is a researched *recommendation* [E13][E19].
3. Approach reachable shore/boat spot without inventing fish spawns, weather percentages or guaranteed bites. Equip **special artificial lure**, cast near active water, retrieve carefully, set hook at strong strike, resist active struggle, then reel/pull during quiet phases [E12][E13].
4. On landing, inspect **actual fish name**, **keep**, and whether the fish routes to **satchel** or **horse**. Many large legendary catches require physical horse transportation. **Do not confuse** an ordinary fish bearing the same species name with its Legendary variant [E13][E14].
5. Ride carefully to an actual **post office** and **send the named fish to Jeremy Gill** using relevant mailing interface. Confirm hand-in/quest progress *in game*; do not infer a mailed fish from the map cross or a satchel icon [E13][E14][E17].
6. Repeat for **13**, including later-gated regions; expect additional mail/quest stage afterward, but avoid future-story outcome in T0/T1. Route general collection ledger and hand-in edge cases to **D20**, without duplicating an all-collectibles database.

**Special vs ordinary lure cost:** PowerPyx lists **$20 each** for three Special Lures, purchased at the Lagras bait shop after Gill's request [E12]. GameFAQs lists ordinary habitat lures around **$2.50**, basic bait/livestock prices vary by vendor and sources [E09]. Those are guide-reported defaults, **not** proof of current menu price, inventory, discount or funds. Do not infer special tackle from general Lagras shop accessibility.

**Legendary inventory/transport risk:** PowerPyx asserts that a large legendary fish lost off the horse can be **caught again** [E13]. Contemporary anecdotes report a crossed-out fish map icon even when Gill mail count is still incomplete [E20]; the map-cross may mark a catch, not hand-in. If lost, first check **post office/mail status, horse cargo, satchel and mission log**. Recapture at the fishing spot is an evidence-backed possibility, but specific spawn delay/automatic recovery and every edition edge case are **UNKNOWN**. Don't promise Trapper-style legendary pelt recovery for fish (that is a *different* system).

**NOT all "legendary" names are Jeremy's 13:** A separately scripted **Legendary Northern Pike** occurs in a different Stranger strand and is **not** one of Gill's requested 13 [E16][E17]. A final **Legendary Channel Catfish** appears in Gill's later quest context, but GameFAQs and Gamer Guides report it is **not actually catchable** during normal Story Mode; early guide headings "catch a 14th" are misleading. This **does not prevent official 13-fish completion**. Details spoiler-gated in section 9 [E13][E17][E21]. Never send a Chapter 2 player hunting it.
