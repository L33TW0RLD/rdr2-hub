## Activity windows: do not overgeneralize

- **Arrival / camp orientation:** Conversations and camp basics, no assumed ledger/tools/horse purchase/fishing.
- **Post-ledger:** Meaningful upgrades and Pearson crafting preparations *if purchased*. All money/ingredients require real readback.
- **Post-stables:** Horse-care education, bonding and local hunting become especially useful.
- **Post-fishing:** River outings and relevant challenge checks become worthwhile.
- **Approaching chapter exit:** Actual missables and active requests take priority over repeatable world grinding.
- **Urgent story strand:** When a skippable Gold stage becomes active, do not give an unrelated multi-hour expedition.
