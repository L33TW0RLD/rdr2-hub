# RDR2 Story Mode — Mechanics Taxonomy v0.1

**State:** DRAFT — DISCOVERY INVENTORY, NOT RATIFIED OR ADOPTED  
**Checked:** 2026-10-16 · **Agent:** Atlas Researcher · **Spoiler boundary:** Chapter 2 mechanics, future plot excluded  
**Scope:** Single-player/story mode only. This is a *coverage index*, not a claim that every microscopic animation, random encounter, item, recipe or challenge step has been exhaustively catalogued.

## Purpose and reading rule

The 30 **families** below enumerate the major gameplay systems and break them into actionable research subjects. Families are topic addresses, **not** 30 mandatory milestones. A topic's existence in this index neither proves that the player has unlocked it nor means that it is required for official 100%. Detailed sources, edge cases, and actual in-game availability require a separate dossier.

Legend for relevance: **S** survival/play, **C** camp/economy, **W** world/immersion, **P** progression, **O** official 100% component (sometimes only a subset), **G** Gold support, **M** possible missable/chapter-sensitive. These are discovery tags, not verified completion flags. Research depth: **INDEXED** throughout; specific unlock claims are further classified in [unlock map](unlock_and_dependency_map_v0_1.md).

## A — Arthur, survival, combat

### S01 · Vitals, cores and character growth [S, P, O, G]
- Health / Stamina / Dead Eye **cores** vs outer bars; core drain and recovery.
- XP gain; level upgrades; maximum capacity; challenges and reinforced equipment interactions.
- Food, tonics, special consumables; effects, duration, crafting, replenishment.
- Dead Eye activation, targeting, marking, accuracy and story-tied functionality changes.
- Read in-game bars and menu before advising a consumable; don't infer exact stats from a mission replay.

### S02 · Food, condition and rest [S, W]
- Nutrition, hunger/weight, underweight/overweight trade-offs; eating cadence.
- Sleep and time-of-day; resting in camp, rented rooms and temporary wilderness camp.
- Temperature exposure; stamina considerations; dirty clothes/blood, cleanliness, bathing.
- Illness/injury/temporary status effects and when a mission limits recovery.
- Carrying/cooking meat, preserving food resources, inventory caps.

### S03 · Appearance and clothing [S, W, P]
- Wardrobe, saved outfits, temperature ratings and outfits stored on horse.
- Hair, beard growth, barber, grooming, bathing, clothing shops and crafted outfits.
- Masks/bandanas and clothing vs witness identification: **do not** assume guaranteed anonymity.
- Talismans/trinkets, weapon equipment and appearance vs actual gameplay effects.

### S04 · Combat and equipment [S, G, P]
- Weapons: revolvers, pistols, repeaters, rifles, shotguns, bow, throwables and melee.
- Aim, cover, blind fire, hip fire, dual wield, quickdraw, mounted combat.
- Ammo types, weapons condition, gun oil, gunsmith customization and capacity.
- Dead Eye stage-dependent targeting, kills vs marks, accuracy, mission-timer discipline.
- Stealth, enemy detection, takedowns, fist fighting, grapples, disarming, lasso/hogtie.
- Looting enemies and dropping equipment, unique weapons, ammunition replenishment.
- Replay/checkpoint restrictions and medal-specific equipment rules belong to Mission Control.

### S05 · Honor, wanted status and law [S, W, M]
- Positive/negative honor choices, greetings, assistance, donation and fishing-release options.
- Witness detection, wanted areas, identification, disguises limitations, bounty price.
- Law responses, surrender/jail, paying bounties at post offices, pursuit/evasion.
- Reputation/discount interactions; **separate** honor effects from story outcome claims.
- Ethical conflicts: witness killing, theft, animal cruelty, ransom and rescue choices.

## B — Horse and travel

### S06 · Horses and ownership [S, P, O]
- Breed/coat/temperament; health, stamina, speed, acceleration, handling; horse compendium.
- Wild-horse taming, found/stolen horses, bonding ranks, commands and whistle range.
- Grooming, feeding, calming, reviving, horse cores, death and replacement risks.
- Saddle transfers, stable slots, temporary horse vs main horse, horse cargo.
- Saddles/stirrups, crafted equipment, stable buying/selling, horse fences (later gating).
- Carrying two long guns, pelts, carcasses, and choice of hunting gear.
- Max-bond progression is an official Total Completion player requirement, but actual bond is unknown.

### S07 · Navigation, travel and field camps [S, W]
- World map, minimap/compass, routes, markers, navigation and discovered locations.
- Horse routes, roads vs shortcuts, train/stagecoach passenger travel and transport.
- Wilderness camp, cooking, resting, camp placement restrictions and fast travel unlock.
- Weather, daylight, terrain, hostile regions and unexpected wildlife encounters.
- Transporting carcasses, pelts or passengers; safe return from longer excursions.
- 'Fast' route vs immersive sightseeing route is a **player preference**, not a mechanic requirement.

## C — The gang and camp

### S08 · Camp supply operations [C, W]
- Donation box and camp funds vs Arthur's wallet; food, medicine, ammunition supply icons.
- Pearson donations, provisions, butcher counter, meat/carcass delivery and camp meals.
- Resupply purchases, daily upkeep, chores, camp morale indicators.
- Rest, wardrobe, letters, journal and opportunities that occur only at gang camps.
- Player-confirmed funds and stock levels required before spending advice.

### S09 · Camp improvements and facilities [C, P]
- Camp Ledger trigger, prices and upgrade prerequisites.
- Dutch/Arthur lodging, fast travel convenience, medicine/ammo/food wagons.
- Pearson Leather Working Tools prerequisite and later satchel crafting.
- Optional upgrades and visual camp improvements; prioritize by actual menu and need.
- Camp geography and features change across story chapters; don't assume permanent access.

### S10 · Pearson crafting and satchels [C, P]
- Satchel categories and carrying capacities; individual crafting unlock challenges.
- Exact perfect pelts required; tracking what has been donated vs still needed.
- Pearson delivery vs Trapper sale vs keeping cargo on horse.
- Camp decoration crafting, animal parts, unique recipes and tools.
- Legend of the East Satchel progression; no automatic credit for theoretical recipes.

### S11 · Gang interactions and camp routine [W, M]
- Ambient conversations, unique dialogue, story-context interactions and available hours.
- Camp chores: chopping wood, carrying sacks, filling water, feeding horses, etc.
- Companion activities (hunting, fishing, robberies), chapter/marker-sensitive availability.
- Companion item requests and deliveries; gifts and unique rewards.
- Camp table games, songs, campfire stories, scripted events and gang contributions.
- Missable-achievement objectives vs ordinary roleplaying opportunities: distinguish.

## D — Wilderness, materials and crafting

### S12 · Hunting and animal systems [S, C, O]
- Study animal, species identity, quality stars, weapon/ammo matching and clean kills.
- Tracking with Eagle Eye, scents, animal behaviour, calls, bait and predator risk.
- Skinning vs intact carcasses, material types, quality loss, spoilage exceptions.
- Bow/small-game arrows, Varmint Rifle, binoculars, location/habitat and spawn timing.
- Pearson/Trapper/Butcher material routing, hunting requests and compendium studying.
- Ethical/honor, camp contribution, storage and transport considerations.
- Research already exists in old repo but is **RESEARCHED — REVIEW PENDING**, not adopted.

### S13 · Legendary animals, Trapper and Fence crafting [P, O]
- Legendary tracking/encounter availability, unique hide/parts, map clues.
- Trapper garments, legendary garment sets, Fence talismans/trinkets and ingredients.
- Revisit/recovery rules for lost legendary materials (**verify before advising**).
- Distinguish one scripted encounter from an actual player-reported animal kill.
- Official completion has a smaller legendary-animal threshold than total hunting scope.

### S14 · Fishing [S, W, P, O]
- Rod unlock, bait vs lures, tackle, casting, reeling, fish species and habitats.
- Fish size, keeping/releasing, honor, food and XP interactions.
- Legendary fishing quest, special lures, challenge progression and fish compendium.
- Cross-chapter availability: no claim the ordinary rod is already obtained in early Chapter 2.

### S15 · Herbalism, plants and gathering [S, P, O]
- Plant spotting with Eagle Eye, picking, plant identification and map biomes.
- Consumable herbs, tonics, seasonings, poison recipes, crafting ingredient reserve.
- Herbalist challenges, compendium discovery and region restrictions.
- Ordinary cooking plants vs rare crafting/quest requirements.

### S16 · Crafting, recipes and wilderness self-sufficiency [S, C, P, O]
- Campfire recipes: food, tonics, ammo, weapons, bait, horse-care items.
- Recipe pamphlets, learning/reading, available ingredients and crafting menus.
- Cooking/seasoning, potency/quality, limited resource allocation.
- Trapper/Pearson/Fence vs personal-campfire craft destinations.
- Recipes crafted for official Total Completion are **types**, not all recipes.

### S20 · Robbery, theft and outlaw trade [C, W, O, M]

## Cross-system pathways worth exposing to Free Roam Control

1. **Camp-development chain:** Story debt return → Camp Ledger → Leather Working Tools → Pearson satchel recipes → precise hunting materials → inventory capacity. Never recommend buying unavailable tools.
2. **Horse competence chain:** Hosea's stable lesson → ownership, brush and bonding → care, tackle and horse cargo → longer hunting/exploration expeditions.
3. **Naturalist chain:** Spot animal → study → identify quality → select weapon → clean kill → choose Pearson / Trapper / other destination → unlock gear or camp benefits.
4. **Self-sufficiency chain:** Gather plants + hunt → cook/craft → keep cores supplied → longer, safer trips.
5. **Treasure/economy chain:** Find map → follow clues → claim valuable → assess unique/crafting reservation → sell *if safe* → fund a meaningful unlocked upgrade.
6. **World-discovery chain:** Encounter person or clue → optional side activity → local exploration/journal → return to camp with a story to report.
7. **Completionist chain:** Natural gameplay action → possible challenge/Compendium/100% credit → check menu → report confirmed result; never retro-credit from planned actions.
8. **Chapter-risk chain:** Story opportunity/missable detected → pause open-world recommendation → offer a spoiler-aware warning and Mission Control handoff.
