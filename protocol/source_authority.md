# Source authority and evidence

1. Player reports and clear screenshots establish actual gameplay outcomes.
2. `L33TW0RLD/rdr2/campaign/` remains authoritative for live campaign state until explicit migration.
3. `L33TW0RLD/rdr2/missions/` contains existing mission guidance; `guides/` contains research with its own adoption status.
4. `rdr2-hub` holds designs and new research, not independent campaign truth.
5. Mark every assertion CONFIRMED, RESEARCHED, PROPOSED, DISPUTED or UNKNOWN as appropriate. Distinguish game facts from tactics and recommendations.
6. No speculative unlock, item, save, medal, honor change, completion percentage or mission availability is to be recorded as fact.
7. Keep current-mission advice spoiler-aware and future-story spoilers out of default free-roam recommendations.
