# INTERMISSION — Chapter 2 Open-World Choice

**Record:** FR.INTERMISSION.CH2_OPEN · v0.1 · **Status:** CONDITIONAL DESIGN EXAMPLE / DRAFT.
**Purpose:** A contextual menu of meaningful leisure opportunities when Arthur **actually regains free-roam control**. Not an active mission or an assertion that the game is currently safe to roam.
**Spoiler:** T0/T1; no upcoming plot.

## Entry gate

Before showing a suggested outing, check the latest `rdr2/campaign/current_state.md` and any player observation for:
- **Control window:** free roaming, camp-limited, currently in/chained mission, or UNKNOWN.
- **Actually offered story content:** player-confirmed marker/mission, not inferred from the numbered mission shelf.
- **Specific active now-or-never risk:** only if actually triggered and sourced, not merely a chapter-wide possibility.
- **Current interests:** camp, hunting, horses, exploring, story or a curiosity observed moments ago.

When control is **UNKNOWN**, phrase this as a *conditional option set* and ask one useful question only if necessary; don't silently assume Arthur is on the Heartlands road.

## Companion recommendation when free roam is actually available

**PROJECT (optional): Develop the Gang's Camp.** Check what upgrades are really available and choose one purposeful milestone, if the player wants to improve camp. [Camp project](../projects/camp_development_v0_1.md). Ledger/currency/tool status is not assumed.

**OUTING (suggested): Heartlands Hunting Reconnaissance.** One short wildlife-learning ride, if position and available tools make it sensible; rewarding even without pelts. [Outing guide](../outings/heartlands_hunting_recon_v0_1.md).

**DISCOVERY:** If the player finds a stranger, interesting animal or unusual cabin, adapt immediately rather than enforcing the hunt.

**STORY:** A direct option to resume an actually available named mission with the **full** Field Guide v1.1. If a specific active timed mission risk is verified, put the risk first and do not pretend the user has an open intermission.

**Alternatives:** camp conversation/chores when available, or simply ride and explore. No forced money target or mandatory hunting quota.

## End condition

Player says where they went, what they learned, or that they are switching to Story. Any reported donation, upgrade, item, Honor, medal or save goes through GameGuide verification; a clicked outing card is not evidence.
