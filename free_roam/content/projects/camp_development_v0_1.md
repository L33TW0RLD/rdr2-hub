## Why it matters

The camp can gradually become a more useful home base. Camp improvements give collected materials and ordinary earnings purpose while creating natural reasons to ride, hunt, help people and return to camp.

## Conditional milestones

| Milestone | First observable check | How it advances the project |
|---|---|---|
| Understand camp facilities | Inspect actual camp facilities and visible donation/upgrade options | Shows what can already be done; no imagined Ledger |
| If Ledger closed, learn its ordinary gate | Check Strauss-related debt recovery/deposit and camp box **only when that activity is actually available** | Helps determine whether improvements can be purchased |
| If Ledger open, prioritize one upgrade | Inspect real menu pricing/benefit, player preferences and actual funds | Optional improving a real camp capability |
| If satchels interest the player | Determine whether Pearson Leather Working Tools were actually purchased, and which recipe/materials are shown | Connects a future hunting outing to crafting without assuming unlocks |

**Reference:** The original mission shelf lists $225 for Leather Working Tools and a $545 optional lodging-to-fast-travel route. These are research/reference prices, not current wallet balances, required spend, or universal menu assertions. Verify actual menu before a purchase.

## Optional outing — Heartlands reconnaissance

If Arthur can roam the Heartlands, use a short local wildlife outing to learn studying animals, hunting quality and material transport. No mandatory number of pelts. See [Heartlands hunting reconnaissance](../outings/heartlands_hunting_recon_v0_1.md).

**Alternate:** Stay around camp, talk to gang members and perform available chores. A fresh story mission is also a legitimate choice when the player wants momentum.

## Safe adaptation

A new stranger, camp conversation or animal can interrupt the outing with no penalty to this project. A credible **active** timed mission must outrank optional camp plans. Do not assume the player has the Ledger, has enough money, or has crafted anything.
