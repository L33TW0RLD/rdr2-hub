## Highest-priority conclusions for coordinator review

1. **Official 100% is a subset target**, including **48 weapon types** and **10 equipment**. It does not require every unique weapon, outfit, trinket or temporary-region species. Individual named guns can nevertheless be irreplaceable for **physical all-items**.
2. **Original pickup vs replay:** a replay may update a named Compendium entry but must not be assumed to restore the physical rare weapon to normal Story inventory.
3. **Unique weapon windows:** protect named Gunslinger drops and later-story Rare Rolling Block original-encounter pickup. World encounters (Rare Shotgun, special Dagger) are **conditional**, not universal chapter-deadline guns.
4. **Talisman components:** protect exact special reward inputs (Lion Paw and Old Brass Compass examples) rather than extending Rockstar's limited lost-legendary-pelt recovery claim to all rare items.
5. **Compendium vs animal trophies:** temporary-region animals can matter to a personally literal catalog yet be **excluded** from Zoologist/Skin Deep. Study, track, kill, skin and discover are not the same claim.
6. **Version-specific catalogs:** Rockstar added Story guns and trinkets in staged PC/PS4/Xbox releases and patched inaccessible platform Compendium rows; no universal item denominator or Special Edition entitlement.
7. **Preservation-first, not chore-first:** a genuine named dropped item merits a timely warning; do not force the player to sweep rare items before ordinary Chapter 2 exploration.
