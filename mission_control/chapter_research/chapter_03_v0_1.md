## 3. Chapter progression graph and closing gates (T2)

**Open early parallel branches** generally include Dutch's `The New South` and Pearson/Sadie's `Further Questions of Female Suffrage` after camp move. GameFAQs reports `Further Questions` may require riding slightly away and returning; `American Distillation` after `The New South` with ~three in-game hour wait. `The Course of True Love I` opens after The New South and feeds parts II→III. `An Honest Mistake` follows **True Love II OR American Distillation**, not rigidly both. [S2]

**Intermediate conditionals**: `Preaching Forgiveness as He Went` reported after An Honest Mistake and two out of three preceding branches (American Pastoral Scene Ch2 / True Love III / American Distillation). `Friends in Very Low Places` after Further Questions + Magicians for Sport and around one in-game hour (GameFAQs). `Blessed are the Peacemakers` after Fine Joys of Tobacco per PowerPyx; `A Short Walk in a Pretty Town` after Friends in Very Low Places plus chapter-wide convergence. These are **source-specific gates**, not exact assumed currently visible markers.

**Hazard chain**: **A Short Walk** begins severe end-Chapter-3 escalation; then **Blood Feuds** and **The Battle of Shady Belle**, the latter reported to **auto-start** after Blood Feuds. Optional Chapter 3 Strauss debts, Beau/Penelope Honor strand, companion activities, camp gifts, limited documents and unique gear should be checked **before** starting the end-chain **when player values those goals**. This is an **evidence-backed preflight**, not instruction to complete every optional world collectible. [S2,S5,S7,S9]

**Last-checklist source distinction:** `Money Lending IV` and `Course of True Love I–III` are **optional Honor Story** toward `Lending a Hand`, not ordinary white-question-mark Stranger strands (D18); camp companion outings have a separate achievement (D05). Their normal Story replay/reward restoration limitations belong to D24. Exact earliest disappearance may be **earlier than actual camp departure** and is source/marker dependent; no claimed universal invisible deadline.
