## 2. Universal Gold accounting, spoiler gates, saving and approved presentation

- **All criteria in one attempt.** A successful single Gold medal is awarded for meeting every listed objective *together* in the specific Story entry; checkmarks obtained across replays do not combine. Gold Rush needs **70** Story medals, while the user's **personal** all-Gold standard conventionally aims at **104**. Official Total Completion is a different list of conditions [S01,S05,S06].
- **Medal result ≠ original-world state.** Replay via Pause → Progress → Story can improve mission medal and checklists but is **not** a safe method to restore first-run Honor choices, previously missed documents, special equipment or requests into the main-world inventory. Retain a protective manual pre-mission save where feasible and independently check Story medal vs actual save slot; [Atlas D24](https://github.com/L33TW0RLD/rdr2-hub/blob/main/systems_atlas/dossiers/d24_replay_saves_medals_v0_1.md) is **RESEARCHED — REVIEW PENDING**, not canonical. Do not assume replay changes original plot result [S06,S07].
- **Patch-specific replay weapon correction:** Older 2018/2019 PowerPyx/RDR2.org guides report generic preset weapons/no tonics in replay. **Rockstar Title Update 1.15** explicitly improved Story Replay to provide **all currently owned weapons** from current Story progression. This does **not** establish identical consumables, horse, core gauge or script-specific loadouts on every platform. Check the player's installed build/loadout before applying archived weapon advice [S07].
- **Gold no-health-item wording:** A prohibition on **health-restoring** items is not blanket prohibition on every Dead Eye-only restorative, but items with combined Health recovery should be treated as unsafe unless their in-game effect is verified. Do not invent owned stocks. No-health restrictions appear on EP2-03/04/06/07/11, not on each of the eleven.
- **Chronology and story experience:** On a first immersive playthrough, default to viewing dialogue/cutscenes. Offer Gold-focused skipping **only as an optional strategy**, and explain proven skip-trigger anomalies for EP2-02 and disputed EP2-09. Do not pressure the player to spoil a reunion/proposal/finale for efficiency. A mission's in-game medal screen is ultimate success evidence, not a website stopwatch.
- **Approved original [Field Guide v1.1](https://github.com/L33TW0RLD/rdr2/blob/main/protocol/walkthrough_presentation.md):** When transferred, render **all actual A→final chronological phases** in a single current-mission response, Gold contract tiles, a computed checklist-progress rail, one inline warning at the right moment, independent phase Intel drawers with source/confidence footers, and simple medal selector/report at end.
