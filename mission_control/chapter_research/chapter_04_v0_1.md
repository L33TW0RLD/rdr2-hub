## 3. Gold-medal contract rules shared by every entry

1. **Every discrete objective must be met in the same single attempt**; repeat only one failed objective on replay does not combine Gold across sessions. The medal result and actual requirement checks appear in `Pause > Progress > Story` [S01][S03][S09].
2. **Time limits are mission-wide unless wording defines a local interval**: 5:30 is a total mission deadline; "within 1:45 after wagon chase" belongs to that **specific chase**, not full mission; "after cover blown, leave in 35s" begins at exposure [S01][S02].
3. **Accuracy/headshots** are independent constraints; use deliberate shots, minimize blind suppression and track shown result. Restriction `only sidearms` means **do not rely on rifle/repeater/shotgun** for kill-credit stages, even when a mission temporarily equips one [S01][S02][S05].
4. **No health items** applies to consumable use for the whole mission, not "finish with a full Health core"; it is safer to avoid ambiguous **multi-core** tonics whose health effect could violate the medal even when taken for Dead Eye. Prepare before starting, and consult D09 consumables read-only [S01][S09].
5. **Checkpoint restart, skip and replay** can affect timers/objective eligibility; never promise checkpoint replay refreshes failure flags. Mission replay changes performance record, **not automatically original-world inventory/Honor/optional choices**. Manual save presence, slot selection and reload persistence always need GameGuide/player verification [S01][S09].
